Lesson vouchers

A voucher is a prepaid package of lessons. The person using it picks their own dates and times, whenever suits.

Buy a voucher

Choose 5 or 10 lessons and the lesson length, then pay online. The voucher is emailed to you, ready to print or forward.

Change the name or message on a voucher

Bought a voucher and want to update who it's for, or what it says? You can do it here in under a minute. Only the person who bought it can make changes, so we'll ask for your voucher code and the email you paid with. The lessons, price and expiry date stay exactly the same.
